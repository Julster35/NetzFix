# DEHSt 2024 Auctioning Reports

## For future agent
Official reports of the German EU ETS allowance (EUA) auctions on EEX, one per quarter of 2024, each with auction-by-auction and **monthly volume-weighted average prices**. Source for the 2024 CO2 price in plant costs (step 2, unit-specific redispatch costs and market model). Auction prices track the EEX spot market closely but are auction clearing prices, not daily spot settlements.

## Claims (`stated`, Q4 2024 report, PDF p. 3 unless noted)
- Q4 2024: about 21 million EUA, almost € 1.36 billion, **volume-weighted average € 65.52** (−3% vs Q3).
- Monthly averages: October € 63.24, November € 66.89, December € 66.66.
- 2024 total (same report): about 85 million EUA and EUAA, about € 5.53 billion, average about € 65 per allowance.
- Q1-Q3 monthly values: to read from the held Q1-Q3 PDFs before use.
