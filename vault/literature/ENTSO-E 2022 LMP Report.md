# ENTSO-E 2022 LMP Report

## For future agent
ENTSO-E's report on the LMP study that produced the nodal prices and the dataset used by [[Knoerr Bichler Dobos 2025]]. It documents market and grid assumptions and the simulation chain for Continental Europe. Needed to interpret the [[BZR dataset]] for cross-validation.

## Claims (`stated`, from the PDF)
- Contents (p. 3): sections on climate-year selection, market assumptions, grid assumptions (Sec. 2.2), simulation chain (2.3) and results (2.4) for Continental Europe and Ireland, and for the Nordic region.
- Introduction (p. 4): the BZR process follows Article 14(3) of Regulation (EU) 2019/943; TSOs delivered data in three steps per Annex II of ACER's BZR methodology decision.
