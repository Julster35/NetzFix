# Xiong et al 2025

## For future agent
Describes and validates the OpenStreetMap-based European high-voltage grid (>= 200 kV) that PyPSA-Eur uses as `base_network: osm`; it is NetzFix's network layer. Licence ODbL 1.0. Held version is the arXiv v1 preprint; the Scientific Data version of record (DOI from a web search, 2026-11-03) is not held.

## Claims (`stated`, from the PDF)
- Abstract (p. 1): a representation of the European high-voltage grid "including and above 200 kV, based on public data provided by OpenStreetMap"; "published under the Open Data Commons Open Database (ODbL 1.0) licence"; the dataset and workflow are part of PyPSA-Eur; validated against official statistics and model runs with different grid representations.
