# SMARD 2025 Netzengpassmanagement 2024

## For future agent
The Bundesnetzagentur's SMARD article on congestion management in 2024 (published 2025-04-02), held as a saved HTML page. It breaks the 2024 total of 30,304 GWh into market-plant redispatch, reserve plants and countertrading, gives renewables curtailment by technology, upward redispatch by fuel and Q4 values. It supplies the calibration target and the out-of-sample tests in [[Validation]].

## Claims (all `stated`, from the saved page)
- Total congestion-management volume 2024: 30,304 GWh (2023: 34,297 GWh), about -12%. (The Monitoringbericht 2025 says ~30,320 GWh: [[BNetzA BKartA 2025 Monitoringbericht]] p. 23.)
- Adjusted feed-in of market plants in redispatch: about 22,777 GWh (2023: 27,068); of this 14,454 GWh reductions; 9,374 GWh by redispatch with renewables (2023: 10,479).
- Upward: feed-in of market plants increased by 8,322 GWh in total (2023: 11,021); gas 4,151 GWh and hard coal 3,792 GWh most often increased.
- Most curtailed renewables: offshore wind 4,562 GWh, onshore wind 3,384 GWh; solar 1,389 GWh (+97% vs 2023).
- Countertrading 5,739 GWh (2023: 6,016). Reserve plants 1,788 GWh (2023: 1,214).
- Q4 2024 total measures 10,424 GWh (Q4 2023: 8,727), of which offshore wind redispatch 1,812 GWh, reserve plants 797 GWh.
- Costs (preliminary): total about 2,776 M EUR; conventional redispatch 1,125; renewables compensation 554; reserve plants about 1,028; countertrading 69. Curtailing renewables about 200 EUR/MWh; conventional redispatch about 142 EUR/MWh.
- 96.5% of renewable generation could be transported to final consumers in 2024.
