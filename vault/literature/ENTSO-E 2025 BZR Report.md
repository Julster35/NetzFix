# ENTSO-E 2025 BZR Report

## For future agent
ENTSO-E's main report of the Bidding Zone Review (target year 2025). It assesses alternative splits of DE-LU (DE2-DE5) and finds DE5 has the highest economic efficiency gain, 339 M EUR, within a range of 251-339 M EUR for all DE-LU splits. NetzFix takes its DE2 and DE5 zone maps from this report ([[Zonal Pricing and Bidding Zones]]). Grey literature, held.

## Claims (`stated`, from the PDF)
- Table 1 (p. 6): "Alternative BZ configurations to be assessed in the BZ Study according to ACER decision 11/2022"; includes Germany-Luxembourg DE4 and DE5 (text extraction; full table to re-read).
- p. 8: economic efficiency gains vs status quo for DE-LU splits "between 251million and 339million for the 2025 target year", "the DE5 split configuration performs the best". Table rows read: DE5 339 (accepted), DE5+NL2 332, DE2 264, DE3 251.
