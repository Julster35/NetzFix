# BNetzA BKartA 2025 Monitoringbericht

## For future agent
The regulators' annual monitoring report covering 2024; NetzFix's main held source of real 2024 figures for validating the playground (congestion management, trade, grid length, peak load). Page numbers below are PDF pages (printed page = PDF page - 1 in this section).

## Claims (all `stated`, from the PDF)
- p. 18: TSO circuit length 38.4 thousand km in 2024; DSO circuit length ~1.9 million km; annual peak load 75.8 GW on 15 January 2024, 11:30-11:45 (2023: 73.7 GW).
- p. 22: system-service costs 2024 ~5.2 bn EUR; congestion management (redispatch with market and reserve plants + countertrading) ~2.9 bn EUR (2023: 3.3 bn); grid losses 1.6 bn; balancing capacity 0.5 bn.
- p. 23: total congestion-management volume 2024 ~30,320 GWh (2023: 34,298 GWh), -12%.
- p. 23: realised cross-border trade 2024: 102.5 TWh, of which exports 37.6 TWh and imports 64.9 TWh; Germany a net importer (as in 2023).
- p. 119: definition: redispatch = operators order plants before a congestion down and behind it up; countertrading = TSOs trade on the intraday market against the flow direction.
- p. 122 (Abbildung 55): 2024 cost split incl. redispatch 1,781 M EUR, countertrading 98 M EUR, reserve plants (holding + use) 1,050 M EUR (chart text; re-read before quoting).
- p. 131: trade flows differ from physical flows because of loop/transit flows, losses, cross-border redispatch and measurement tolerances.
- p. 135 (Abbildung 66): exchange schedules vs physical flows per border, chart only (no numbers in the text layer).
