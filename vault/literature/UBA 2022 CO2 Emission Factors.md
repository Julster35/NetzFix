# UBA 2022 CO2 Emission Factors

## For future agent
Official German fuel-related CO2 emission factors (the ones used in the National Inventory Report), in t CO2 per TJ of fuel. NetzFix uses them for the CO2 part of plant marginal cost ([[Assumptions]] A30).

## Claims (`stated`, Table 23, PDF pp. 52-53, column 2020)
- Raw hard coal (power stations, industry): 93.6 t CO2/TJ.
- Raw lignite, public power stations: Rheinland 113.3, Lausitz 110.2, Mitteldeutschland 103.6 t CO2/TJ.
- Natural gas, Germany: 55.8 t CO2/TJ.
- Heavy fuel oil: 79.7; light heating oil: 74.0 t CO2/TJ.
- Conversion used in code: 1 MWh = 0.0036 TJ.
