# EWI 2025 Policy Brief Gebotszonensplit

## For future agent
EWI's literature-based policy brief on splitting the DE-LU bidding zone into two. It weighs static and dynamic effects and concludes that a firm quantitative welfare assessment is not possible yet; welfare gains might be outweighed by losses. Held (copy from the Bonn university library deposit). Author names from a web search, to confirm on the PDF title page.

## Claims (`stated`, from the PDF)
- p. 4: analyses possible effects of a two-way split of the DE-LU bidding zone on economic welfare, summarising static and dynamic arguments from current research; reduced liquidity and administrative switching costs "in Milliardenhöhe" could reduce welfare.
- p. 5: "eine abschließende quantitative Bewertung der Wohlfahrtseffekte eines Gebotszonensplits [ist] derzeit nicht möglich"; per current research, welfare gains could be outweighed by welfare losses (sentence continues beyond the extract; re-read).
