# Hoersch et al 2018

## For future agent
The original PyPSA-Eur paper: an open model dataset of the European transmission system with network, power plants, demand and renewable availability. NetzFix builds on today's PyPSA-Eur (v2026.09.0), whose network is now OSM-based ([[Xiong et al 2025]]), so this paper describes the model's origin, not its current network. Held version is the arXiv preprint.

## Claims (`stated`, from the PDF)
- Abstract (p. 1): "the first open model dataset of the European power system at the transmission network level to cover the full ENTSO-E area"; 6001 lines (AC >= 220 kV and all HVDC), 3657 substations, an open database of conventional power plants, demand and variable renewable availability time series; suitable for operational and expansion studies.
